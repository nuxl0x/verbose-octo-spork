[VOS] verbose-octo-spork \ Manual \ uninstall
=======================================
Syntax: (vos) uninstall

The purpose of the uninstall command is self explanatory, as in the title.
This command will completely remove VOS from your system by removing associated VOS files in the following locations:
- '/usr/local/bin/vos'
- '/usr/local/share/vos'

These are the folders that contain the majority of VOS and after removing them the command successfuly will have removed VOS.
After uninstallation, both the uninstall command and any other VOS commands will become unavailable.
