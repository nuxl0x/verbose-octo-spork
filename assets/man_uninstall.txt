[VOS] verbose-octo-spork \ Manual \ uninstall
=======================================
Syntax: (vos) uninstall

This command is very simple, and will uninstall VOS by deleting it from your system entirely. 
Effectively, this command deletes both the executable in /usr/local/bin and the share in /usr/local/share.
